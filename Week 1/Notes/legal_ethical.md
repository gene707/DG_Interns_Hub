# Legal and Ethical Basics

## Authorization & Scope
- **Authorization**: Written permission from the asset owner before any testing. Without it, activity is illegal.
- **Scope**: Clearly defined boundaries (IPs, domains, systems, time windows) that may be tested.

## Rules of Engagement (RoE)
- Define allowed tools, techniques, and data handling.
- Specify reporting format and timelines.
- Include **no‑disclosure** clauses for sensitive findings.

## Cyber‑Crime Law (Country‑Specific)
- In the United States, the **Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)** criminalizes unauthorized access to computers.
- In the European Union, the **Directive on attacks against information systems** (EU 2013/40) covers similar offenses.
- Mention any local legislation relevant to your jurisdiction (e.g., India’s **IT Act 2000**, Australia’s **Criminal Code**).

> [!TIP] Always keep a copy of the signed engagement contract before starting any lab work that mimics real‑world testing.
