# SOC Analyst, VAPT/Penetration Tester, Incident Responder Comparison

| Role | Goal | Daily Work | Common Tools | Team (Blue/Red) |
|------|------|------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **SOC Analyst** | Detect & monitor security events | Review alerts, investigate incidents, create tickets, tune SIEM | Splunk, ELK, QRadar, Wireshark, OSSEC | Blue |
| **VAPT / Penetration Tester** | Find exploitable weaknesses before attackers | Recon, scanning, exploitation, reporting | Nmap, Burp Suite, Metasploit, Nessus, OWASP ZAP | Red |
| **Incident Responder** | Contain, eradicate, recover from an active breach | Triage alerts, isolate systems, forensic collection, remediation | Volatility, TheHive, Cortex, Sysinternals, GRR | Blue |
